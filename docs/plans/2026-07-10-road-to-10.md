Each numbered stage ends with the same discipline as today: adversarial review + live validation before it ships.

| **HONESTY RULE (non-negotiable):** every forecast carries uncertainty bands, declared assumptions, and the 0.4%-coverage denominator caveat in-UI | "proyecciones honestas" as the differentiator nobody else has | — | doctrine |
