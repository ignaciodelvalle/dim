### Public open-data downloads apply k=5 plus complementary AND cross-dataset joint suppression
- **Property**: no province cell, and no arithmetic difference between published cells and the published national total, can recover a count below `ANONYMITY_K` — including by joining two datasets sharing a population base.
- **Path**: `lib/open-data/province-suppression.ts:88`
- **Guard**: `lib/open-data/__tests__/province-suppression.test.ts`, `lib/open-data/__tests__/datasets.test.ts`, `app/(public)/transparencia/__tests__/privacy-disclosure-parity.test.ts` — refuter verified the guard directly (`toBe(ANONYMITY_K)` derivation-checked, symmetric nested-base tests at `datasets.test.ts:292,311,403`).
- **What would break it**: redefining `OPEN_DATA_K` as its own literal instead of `= ANONYMITY_K`; emitting `0` instead of `SUPPRESSED_MARKER` for a withheld cell; adding a sixth dataset over a shared population base without registering it in the joint-suppression group.
- **Evidence**: `rg -n 'ANONYMITY_K|OPEN_DATA_K|complementarySuppress|WITHHELD|JOINT|base column' lib/open-data/province-suppression.ts lib/open-data/datasets.ts` → `province-suppression.ts:88 export const OPEN_DATA_K = ANONYMITY_K;`; `:100` complementary-suppression group is the whole country; `datasets.ts:476-478` documents the JOINT rule across shared-base datasets.

- **A06-G1** — `notification_dead_letter.payload` stores a third party's name and phone/e-mail verbatim, forever, and no erasure or lifecycle job ever reaches it. Path: `lib/infra/notification-service.ts:265`. `report-pet-sighting.ts:327-329` folds an anonymous finder's `finderName`/`finderContact` into the notification body before `createNotificationsBulk`; on an insert failure, `deadLetter()` (`notification-service.ts:253-267`) writes the whole `values` object — including that body — verbatim to `notification_dead_letter.payload`.
