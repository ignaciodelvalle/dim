### 4. The residual de-anonymisation channel on denuncias

An anonymous denuncia filed from the phone writes **no** uuid into
`welfare_reports` and **does** write the caller's uuid into
`rate_limit_buckets`, as the key `welfare_auth:{userId}:hour:{window}`
(`2fe159ba8`). What was accepted, fully derived in
`app/api/v1/welfare-reports/commands.ts`: the table is deny-all under RLS with
no policy and no grant ever written, no product surface reads it, and the row
expires within the hour. What that rests on: the reading that anonymity here is
"a property of the RECORD, not unattributability in flight" — a narrow reading
of a promise made to somebody filing a criminal allegation, and the web's own
anonymous door behaves identically. It is PO item 7 on the board because
closing it is a flood-control trade on a legal filing: either no per-user
ceiling on the anonymous path, or per-IP pricing (the web's `welfare_anon` at
1/min · 3/hr per address is one denuncia per carrier gateway per minute).

The WORSE sibling channel found in the same window was **closed in code, not
handed to you**: a limiter driver failure threw the bucket key — uuid included
— into Vercel's function logs, which have no RLS, no TTL and a wide reader
population. `redactCallerId` closes it and reverting it turns a test red
(`3900bdbb5`).
