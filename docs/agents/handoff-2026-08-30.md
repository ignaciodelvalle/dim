## C. The decisions and their why — ratify or revert

Every entry is live behaviour today. "Ratify" costs a nod; "revert" costs a
small, named change. None of them should stay a side effect of nobody asking.

1. **Denunciar last in the footer** (`eb9784090`). Argument: the footer orders
   by distance from what the reader is responsible for, and a criminal
   allegation about somebody else's animal is where that line ends — plus
   physical separation so a thumb aiming at Adoptar cannot file one. Revert is
   a one-line reorder; the render-tree test will name the new arrangement.
2. **Geocoder outage = 503, not "no encontramos esa dirección"**
   (`3cdb49cdb`). Argument: an empty list tells somebody standing in front of
   an injured animal that the street they are looking at does not exist, and
   they retype until they give up. Cost: the flow stops instead of degrading.
   The wrong fix is pinned by a test, so reverting is a decision, not a slip.
3. **"Próximos" closes at `ends_at` on the phone, and the web still buckets on
   `starts_at`** (`a7a18f826`). Argument: the check-in QR lives until
   `ends_at`, so the web files a turno under "Pasados" while its QR is still
   valid — somebody arriving late looks under the wrong heading. The two
   surfaces DISAGREE today; the real decision is whether to migrate the web
   page (a browser-facing change with its own e2e gate), not whether to revert
   the phone.
4. **The signup triple 60/min · 180/hr · 360/day** (`d2a190e78`). Ratify the
   NUMBERS, not just the shape — 360 accounts/day/gateway is what a patient
   farm gets for free, and the derivation says out loud that no arrangement of
   windows fixes signup until email or phone verification exists (the former is
   your Resend item).
5. **Export fails CLOSED, erasure fails OPEN** (`d9bc3672a`, WU-R). Argument: a
   limiter outage must not enable an unbounded PII dump, and an abuse control
   must not stand between a person and a legal right. This is the pair to
   ratify together; each direction alone sounds wrong.
6. **A DEACTIVATED account is refused both ARCO rights on the phone and granted
   both on the web** (WU-R block on the board). The read half is argued in a
   docblock; the WRITE half — art. 16, the right to be erased — is denied on
   one surface and allowed on the other **with no written reason**. This one is
   not ratify-or-revert, it is DECIDE: the board explicitly marks it a PO
   decision nobody has taken.
7. **The claim dispute is refused on the phone, not deferred** (`39377eedd`,
   WU-V). Argument: your own 2026-07-30 decision requires an evidence FILE, a
   JSON door cannot carry one, and a `dispute` command would be a control the
   server refuses on every tap. The screen sends people to the browser. Ratify
   unless you want the evidence rule itself revisited.
8. **`petToken` travels only for `lost` claims — one step tighter than the
   web's own action** (WU-V). Argument: a token is a navigable capability and
   travels only where the client has a destination. The web returns one for
   `free` and `active_owner` too; the divergence is deliberate and recorded.
9. **The phone is stricter than the web on DEACTIVATED for claims, booking,
   moves and returns** (WU-V, WU-P). Argument: granting nothing the browser
   grants is the safe direction, and every instance is pinned by a test so it
   stays a decision. The web's own looseness (no limiter on four server
   actions, `requireUserOrRedirect` passing DEACTIVATED) is reported as the
   WEB'S gap each time.
10. **Booking refusals fold onto existing error codes, and two folds survive on
    purpose** (`a1599aaef`). `pet_not_yours`/`pet_deceased` share one code so
    the booking door is not an existence oracle over erased pets; `slot_past`
    still borrows `appointment_past`, whose es-AR copy ends in "no se puede
    cancelar" — a person refused a BOOKING reads a sentence about cancelling.
    The fourth code and its string are written down and waiting; say yes and it
    is a small change.
11. **No length cap was invented over `pets.name` / `pets.color`**
    (`0904a7105`). Argument: the columns are unbounded, longer values already
    exist, and a cap applied on the way back out locks an owner out of
    correcting the COLOUR because the form posts both fields. Caps gate only
    NEW values.
12. **The phone never derives an authorization flag the server owns** —
    `canClaim`, `canBook`, `section`/`canCancel`/`canCheckIn`, the return
    screen's `capabilities` (WU-V, WU-S, WU-P). Argument: a client deriving
    "free to claim" from adjacent data is how a control gets drawn that the
    write refuses. The pattern is pinned by contradiction tests (a `free` ack
    with `canClaim: false` must draw no button). Ratify the pattern once and it
    binds future lanes.

Two PRE-EXISTING decisions this range leaned on without re-opening, listed so
you know they carried weight again: species/jurisdiction stay FULL-LOCK on the
