**Method:** Sequential, one session per role (Chrome), desktop ~1280–1560px. Every role logged in, every major route walked. **No side-effectful submissions** (no forms submitted, no approvals/verifications/overrides fired) — create/manage flows were inspected structurally only.

## Verdict

This does not read like a resurrected 2021 student project — it reads like a public-health-grade civic platform. The event-sourced model is coherent across every role (the same Firulais rabies case surfaces in the owner libreta, the govt case list, and the admin observations queue), the authorization model is genuinely best-in-class, and privacy is engineered and disclosed (k-anonymity, PII-search audit, Tier-0 public credential). The gaps that remain are overwhelmingly **polish and scale**, not architecture — with one real functional bug.

---

## Top cross-cutting issues (ranked)

1. **🔴 Adoptions/Operaciones crashes (org portal).** `/org/[org]/adopciones` throws a Server-Components error boundary ("Algo salió mal", digest e.g. 372514334) on the shelter that has live adoptions ("2 en curso"); it renders fine on orgs with none. Data-dependent crash on active-adoption rendering. Blocks a shelter's core job. *(org-admin)*

2. **Incomplete localization (most pervasive).** Raw enums/English leak across govt, org, and admin: species `dog`/`cat`/`guinea_pig`/`rabbit`, disease `lepto`/`rabies_suspected`, UI words `PETS`/`Signals`; plus missing Spanish accents (`investigacion`, `verificacion`, `JURISDICCION`, `ACCION`, `Metodos`). One i18n/enum map would fix most of it.

3. **Metric consistency & self-contradiction.** "Cobertura antirrábica" = **42%** (panel/panorama) vs **54%** (analytics/vigilancia) under one label; "SLA ENO **100%**" shown "Normal" next to "**12 en breach**"; owner pet header "**AL DÍA**" vs "**0 DE 3 AL DÍA**" compliance. One canonical definition per KPI.

4. **Console lists don't scale (admin).** The global audit log is buried under ~150 identical bulk-override rows; Gobiernos (~55 rows) and Usuarios (50) are dominated by seed accounts with no search/filter/pagination. Real design gap independent of seed noise.

5. **Menu/permission gating gap (vet).** A vet without `bite.report` can still open the full "Reportar mordedura" create form, contradicting the stated "cada permiso habilita su módulo en el menú." Gate the route or hide the item.

6. **Navigation polish (owner).** Client-side navigation retains scroll position (credential/account pages open scrolled mid/bottom); recurring large trailing whitespace; a breadcrumb mismatch ("← MIS MASCOTAS" on the denuncias page).

7. **Label inconsistencies.** One abuse module is "Maltrato" (sidebar) / "Bienestar" (topbar) / "Investigaciones de maltrato" (title); `/admin/usuarios` mislabeled "MIMAR GOBIERNO"; sidebar "Mascotas" opens "Animales en custodia".

8. **Data-integrity display.** Future-dated events surface as real ("último evento 15/10/26", "31/12/26"; outbreak "PICO 14 oct 2026"); "Top 5 / Top 10" headings render 3 / 5 rows; mixed case-code casing.

9. **Identity/greeting bug (vet).** Personal home greets "Buen día, **Dra..**" (doubled period, title instead of name) while the org portal correctly shows "Dra. Lilian Marrone."

**Performance (all roles):** org and govt pages were consistently slow to paint (repeated 30s screenshot timeouts in dev). Worth a production Lighthouse/profiler pass on `/org/*` and `/gob/*`.

## What's genuinely excellent (keep / lean in)

- **Capability model + "Tus permisos"** — per-capability code, plain-language description, granted state, self-service request; admin sees "TODOS LOS PERMISOS" + approval queue. Best-in-class authorization transparency.
- **Privacy by construction** — Tier-0 public credential (no owner PII), granular owner privacy toggles tied to Ley 25.326, k-anonymity suppression with disclosed hidden-cell counts, and a **"Buscó por PII" audit trail**.
- **Legal grounding everywhere** — Ley 5470, 14.346, 14.107, 4078; Decreto 4669/1973; indicator codes A7–A12 / B3–B9; ISO 11784/11785 chip validity.
- **Event-sourced coherence** — one rabies case renders consistently across owner, govt, and admin.
- **Domain-rich, welfare-first UX** — natural-language libreta logging, contextual rabies-observation warnings, severity taxonomy on abuse denuncias, post-adoption check-ins, Panorama's temporal replay + question-framed views, provenance stamps ("REGISTRADO POR VOS").
- **Good error/empty/404 design** — friendly, coded, with escape actions.

## Suggested fix order

1. Fix the adoptions crash (functional blocker).
2. One i18n pass (species/disease enums + accents) — highest visible-polish ROI, spans 3 portals.
3. Reconcile contradictory KPIs (antirrábica 42/54, SLA ENO, AL DÍA vs 0/3).
4. Add filter/pagination to admin consoles + make the audit log scannable.
5. Owner nav polish (scroll-to-top, whitespace, breadcrumb) + vet greeting + menu-gating.
6. Label unification + future-date validation.
